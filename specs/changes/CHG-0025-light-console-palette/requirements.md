## VISUAL-001 — Light canvas and surfaces

WHEN any control-center view is open
THE SYSTEM SHALL use a light neutral canvas, white primary surfaces, subtle cool borders and
shall not expose a legacy dark sidebar, toolbar, card, modal or editor surface.

## VISUAL-002 — Role-based palette

WHEN the UI renders actions or state
THE SYSTEM SHALL reserve indigo for primary actions and selected navigation, green for healthy
or completed state, blue for active/information state, amber for partial state and red for
failure or destructive actions.

## VISUAL-003 — Quiet hierarchy

WHEN adjacent surfaces are shown
THE SYSTEM SHALL distinguish them primarily through small background and border changes, with
restrained shadows and no decorative high-contrast gradients in routine control surfaces.

## VISUAL-004 — Chat readability

WHEN the Agent conversation is open
THE SYSTEM SHALL keep message text on a white transcript, preserve readable user-message and
composer contrast, and keep the composer controls visually subordinate to the send action.

## VISUAL-005 — Interaction preservation

WHEN the palette is applied
THE SYSTEM SHALL preserve existing chat scrolling, streaming, Plan, Todo, Choice, Trace and
keyboard interaction behavior.
